A 12-metre road along the lake

The Greater Bengaluru Authority's draft circulation plan for Bengaluru North (Grid 7) draws a proposed 12-metre Hebbala - Thanisandra road right beside Hebbal Lake.

See the road drawn on the official plan (PDF, Grid 7, page 8)Source: GBA Proposed Circulation Plan, Bengaluru North City Corporation, Grid 7 (official PDF hosted on OpenCity). The road label appears beside Hebbal Lake on page 8.

  • Proposed 12 m Hebbala - Thanisandra road, drawn along the lake's edge on Grid 7 of the plan.

  • Public suggestions and objections are invited under Notification No. CC/GBA/PR/304/2026-27 (gazette dated 12 October 2026).

  • The notice allows 60 days from publication. Send yours early, don't wait for the last day.
