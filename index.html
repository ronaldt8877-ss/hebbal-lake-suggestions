What is proposed

A 12-metre road along the lake

The Greater Bengaluru Authority's draft circulation plan for Bengaluru North (Grid 7) draws a proposed 12-metre Hebbala - Thanisandra road right beside Hebbal Lake.

See the road drawn on the official plan (PDF, Grid 7, page 8)

Source: GBA Proposed Circulation Plan, Bengaluru North City Corporation, Grid 7 (official PDF hosted on OpenCity). The road label appears beside Hebbal Lake on page 8.

Proposed 12 m Hebbala - Thanisandra road, drawn along the lake's edge on Grid 7 of the plan.
Public suggestions and objections are invited under Notification No. CC/GBA/PR/304/2026-27 (gazette dated 12 October 2026).
The notice allows 60 days from publication. Send yours early, don't wait for the last day.

Why it matters

Lakes are not spare land

Haven't we lost enough of Bengaluru's lakes already? Let's not wait until later to ask what we could have done. Let's do what we can today, for a better tomorrow. Traffic needs attention. Protecting the lake, its trees and its drainage can be part of the solution, not the price of it.
